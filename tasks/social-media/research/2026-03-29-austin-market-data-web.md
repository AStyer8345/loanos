## Key Data Points — February 2026 (Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos MSA)

### Inventory
- Active listings: **~10,000** — down **4.8% year-over-year**
- Sellers returning at roughly the same pace as last year

### Buyer Activity
- Pending sales: **2,690** — up **13.9% year-over-year**
- Buyers returning faster than sellers — market tilting toward less inventory per buyer

### Pricing Signals
- Close-to-list price ratio: **92.0%** in February (up from 90.6% in December 2025)
- Buyers and sellers more aligned than late 2025
- Median list price (Nov 2025 latest confirmed): $499,000

### Market Outlook
- Spring 2026: showing balance — seller inventory flat YoY, buyer demand up
- Market described as "warm" not sizzling — opportunity window for buyers before summer competition
