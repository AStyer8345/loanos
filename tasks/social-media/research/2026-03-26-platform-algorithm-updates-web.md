## LinkedIn Algorithm 2026

**Source:** https://sproutsocial.com/insights/linkedin-algorithm/
**Summary:** LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm prioritizes relevance and professional value over virality. Niche-specific content now outperforms broad "growth hacking" posts. The algorithm rewards content that triggers conversation in your professional network — posts that get comments from people who are themselves credible in your niche get amplified further. Engagement quality (comments > reactions) matters more than volume.

**Source:** https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-algorithm/ (already in notebook)
**Key additions:** Carousels and documents still high performers. Video growing. Posting 2-3x/week optimal. Peak times: Tue-Thu 8-10am.

## Instagram Reels 2026 — Financial Services

**Source:** https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/ (already in notebook)
**New finding:** DM sends are the heaviest-weighted signal for Reels distribution in 2026. Content that gets shared via DM reaches significantly more people. For mortgage: short explainer Reels that people share with friends buying homes.

**Source:** https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/
**Summary:** Instagram's 2026 algorithm is surface-specific — Reels have their own algorithm separate from Feed. Reels are prioritized for reach; Feed posts prioritize engagement from existing followers. For mortgage LOs: use Reels for top-of-funnel awareness (reach new Austin buyers), use Feed posts for nurturing existing followers (rate updates, client wins).

**Source:** https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-benchmarks-for-financial-services/
**Summary:** Financial services benchmarks 2025 (updated): Instagram median engagement rate 0.67%, LinkedIn 0.54%, Facebook 0.15%. Instagram Reels drive 3-5x higher reach than static posts for FinServ. Best posting frequency for FinServ: Instagram 3-5x/week, LinkedIn 2-3x/week, Facebook 3-5x/week.

## Facebook Organic Reach 2026 — Local Business

**Source:** https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/what-facebooks-new-link-rules-mean-for-your-2026-strategy/
**Summary:** Meta's Oct 2025 update changed how external links are treated in the feed. Posts with external links in the caption are now severely deprioritized. Best practice: post native video or images, put links in comments or bio. For mortgage LOs: never put your application link in the post body — put it in first comment or Stories link.

**Key insight for Adam:** Facebook organic reach is ~10% of page followers. Video (Reels) gets 50% more reach after Meta's algorithm update. Local GBP-style posts (neighborhood market updates, Austin-specific) do better than generic mortgage content on Facebook.
