## RESEARCH PROTOCOL

### 1. Industry Benchmarks
Study the best content creators and strategists in the mortgage + financial services space:
- Gary Vaynerchuk's content system — volume, repurposing, platform-native formats
- Alex Hormozi's organic growth strategy — value-first, no-BS content
- Top mortgage LO accounts on LinkedIn: identify top 5 by follower count and engagement
- Top mortgage content on Instagram: identify Reels formats and hook patterns that perform
- Bankrate, NerdWallet, and CFPB social presence — see how financial services firms handle compliance online
- What do the top-performing Austin TX real estate and mortgage accounts post about?

### 2. Competitor Analysis
Review top 3 Austin mortgage LO social media accounts:
- Posting frequency per platform (posts/week)
- Content types (video, text, carousel, static image, Reels)
- Engagement rates (likes, comments, shares relative to follower count)
- Content themes (rate updates, education, testimonials, personal brand)
- What they're doing that Adam isn't
- Gaps Adam can own that competitors aren't covering

### 3. Platform Best Practices
Research current state (not evergreen generalizations) for each platform:

**LinkedIn:**
- Current algorithm weighting (dwell time, early comments, shares vs. reactions)
- Text-only vs. carousel vs. document post performance in 2025/2026
- Best posting times for Austin TX business hours audience
- Hashtag strategy — how many, which types
- Connection request vs. follower strategy for mortgage LOs

**Instagram:**
- Reels vs. static post reach differential in current algorithm
- Story cadence and engagement tactics
- Best practices for link-in-bio tools (Linktree vs. native links)
- Hashtag count and relevance for local financial services
- Audio/music strategy for Reels in financial services (copyright-safe)

**Facebook:**
- Business page organic reach vs. personal profile strategy
- Group participation strategy for local Austin community groups
- Boosted post criteria — what's worth boosting vs. what isn't
- Retargeting pixel setup for website visitors → social audience

### 4. Compliance / Risk
Research and document specific compliance requirements for mortgage social media:
- RESPA Section 8: what constitutes a referral fee in social media context
- FTC guidelines: testimonial disclosures, "#ad" requirements, review authenticity
- Regulation Z: exact rules for advertising specific rates — when APR disclosure is required
- Texas OCCC (Office of Consumer Credit Commissioner): any Texas-specific mortgage advertising rules
- NMLS# 513013 placement requirements — must appear on rate-related posts
- Equal Housing Lender: exact disclosure requirements for visual posts
- Prohibited language: "guaranteed approval", "no credit check", "best rates guaranteed"
- Screenshots/repost rules: can Adam repost client testimonials without FTC disclosure?

### 5. Performance Data — Adam's Current State
Check any available engagement data:
- Native platform insights from LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook (if Adam has shared access)
- Any Buffer or Later analytics available
- Historical posting patterns — when did Adam last post on each platform?
- Which post formats has Adam tried before? What got the most engagement?
- Current follower counts on each platform (estimate if not available)
- What content themes have generated direct messages or leads historically?
