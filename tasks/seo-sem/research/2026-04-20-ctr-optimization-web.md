## 3-Sentence Summary

Backlinko analyzed 4 million Google search results and found that position #1 captures ~27.6% of all clicks, while position #2 gets ~15.8% — meaning even small ranking jumps have outsized CTR impact. Positive sentiment in title tags generates 4.1% higher absolute CTR compared to neutral or negative titles, validating the CTR-hook approach already in use on suburb pages. Google rewrites ~61% of title tags, so titles must align tightly with searcher intent or Google will substitute its own version — making the keyword-first, differentiator-second structure critical.
