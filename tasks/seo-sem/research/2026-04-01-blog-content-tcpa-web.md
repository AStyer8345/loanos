## Topic: Long-Tail Keyword Strategy for Mortgage Blog Content

**Source:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/long-tail-keywords/
**Title:** Long-Tail Keywords: How to Find and Use Them (Ahrefs)
**Summary:** Long-tail keywords are specific search queries with lower monthly volume but significantly higher conversion intent — 91.8% of all Google searches are long-tail. For a mortgage broker blog, targeting phrases like "how to choose a mortgage lender Austin TX" captures buyers further down the funnel with less competition. Ahrefs recommends building content clusters around a head keyword with multiple long-tail variations, which also helps Google understand topical authority.

---

**Source:** https://searchengineland.com/long-tail-keywords-seo-tips-392307
**Title:** 9 Tips to Get the Full SEO Benefits of Long-Tail Keywords (Search Engine Land)
**Summary:** Long-tail keywords in blog content convert better because they match specific user intent — a searcher typing "how do I choose between a mortgage broker and a bank" is ready to make a decision, not just browsing. Search Engine Land recommends structuring each long-tail post around one primary keyword with 2-3 secondary variations naturally integrated, avoiding keyword stuffing. Using FAQ schema on these posts amplifies the chance of appearing in AI-generated answers and featured snippets, even if traditional FAQ rich results are restricted.
