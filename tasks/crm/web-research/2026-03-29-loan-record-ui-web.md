## Key Themes Found

- **Information hierarchy matters**: Top mortgage CRMs separate "deal critical" (borrower name, status, close date, rate, amount) from "reference details" (documents, conditions, contacts, timeline)
- **Pipeline-first views**: Status badges with color coding, urgency indicators, and "what needs action next" surfacing are table stakes
- **Tabbed secondary sections**: Documents, conditions, timeline, and communication history are consistently moved behind tabs or accordions in modern LO CRMs
- **Mobile-aware layouts**: Card-style summaries over dense tables for field use
- **Zero-clutter above the fold**: Top performers show ≤8 fields above the fold on loan records — the rest is on demand
