## Buffer: 2026 Social Media Benchmarks
URL: https://buffer.com/resources/social-media-benchmarks/

Key findings for mortgage LOs:
- Creators posting 20+ weeks out of 26 see ~450% more engagement per post vs. those posting in 4 weeks or fewer — consistency is the single biggest lever
- Platform-native content performs 100x better than generic repurposed clips
- Human-generated content remains the #1 consumer priority; AI should power insights and efficiency, not replace authentic voice

Strategic implication for Adam: The 30-day calendar must ship as a consistent publishing commitment, not a one-off sprint. Platform-native content means LinkedIn carousels ≠ Instagram copy-paste.

## RESPA Social Media Compliance — Key Rules
URL: https://www.activecomply.com/compliance-resources/respa-101-how-mortgage-marketers-can-stay-compliant

Key compliance rules for Adam's social media:
- RESPA Section 8: No fee, kickback, or "thing of value" for referrals — applies to social media likes, shares, and co-marketing arrangements with realtors
- Contests/giveaways tied to referrals are high-risk RESPA violations — avoid "refer a friend" promotions
- Joint social posts with realtors must clearly disclose the relationship and cannot involve unequal cost-splitting (disguised referral payment)
- Every social post is considered an advertisement and must comply with RESPA + state mortgage advertising rules
