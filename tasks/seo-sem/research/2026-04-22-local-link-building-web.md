# Research: Local Link Building for Service Businesses
Date: 2026-04-22
Source: https://ahrefs.com/blog/local-link-building/
Topic: Week 7 prep — Link Building

## Summary
Ahrefs' "9 Easy Local Link Building Tactics" covers practical strategies for local service businesses to build high-quality backlinks without expensive outreach campaigns. Key tactics include: leveraging existing supplier and partner relationships (ask for a link on their "partners" page), getting listed in local directories and aggregator sites that serve as consideration-set resources, and sponsoring local events or organizations for organic press coverage.

## Relevance to styermortgage.com
- Realtor partnership pages are a direct analog — existing relationships with realtors who have websites are a natural link source
- Austin-area directories (Austin Board of Realtors, local business associations, Austin Chamber) are prime targets
- Week 7 of the SEO queue focuses specifically on link building; this article covers the exact tactics needed without requiring a link-buying budget

## Key Tactics to Apply
1. Partner pages — reach out to realtor partners to link styermortgage.com from their sites
2. Local directories — Austin Chamber, ABJ, HAR, TREC-adjacent resources
3. Guest content — write mortgage explainer content for realtor blogs
4. Sponsor local real estate events / first-time buyer seminars for press links
5. Local citations — NAP consistency across Yelp, BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor
