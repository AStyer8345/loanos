## Industry Benchmarks

**LinkedIn Carousels:**
- Carousels earn **278% more engagement than video** on LinkedIn (Buffer, 2026 — supersedes earlier 3x figure from Week 1 PM research)
- Recommended structure: **~12 slides**, 25–50 words per slide, 500 characters per slide maximum
- First slide = hook/headline. Last slide = CTA. Middle slides = value delivery in sequential steps or insights
- Caption formula: max **150 characters** (avoids truncation on mobile) — explain WHY this matters + what reader learns
- Combine visuals with concise bullet points — complex ideas broken into digestible visuals drive highest dwell time
- Reiterate CTA at end of document AND in the caption
- Source: [Buffer LinkedIn Carousels Guide](https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-carousels/) | [Sprout Social LinkedIn Carousels](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/linkedin-carousels/)

**Instagram Algorithm 2026:**
- Instagram removed hashtag following (December 2024) — users can no longer follow hashtags
- **Keywords in captions and bio now matter more than hashtags** for discoverability
- Hashtags still useful for search but not for feed algorithmic distribution
- Source: [Sprout Social Instagram Best Practices 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-best-practices/)

## Platform Best Practices

### LinkedIn

**Carousel Format — Production Spec for Adam:**
- Upload as PDF (not multi-image) for document carousel format
- Slide 1: Hook (bold statement, surprising stat, or provocative question)
- Slides 2–10: One insight per slide, short sentence or 3-bullet structure
- Slide 11: Summary/TL;DR — "Here's what you need to know in one line"
- Slide 12: CTA — "DM me 'RATES' or 'QUALIFY' for a free consult" + NMLS# 513013
- Caption: "I help Austin buyers [specific outcome]. [Topic in one sentence]. Swipe to learn [what they'll get]. ↓" (150 chars max)
- Image: Dark background with gold text works well for financial content (matches LoanOS branding)

**Hashtag Strategy (2026):**
- Use **3–5 hashtags only** — more looks spammy
- Mix: 1 broad + 1 niche + 1 local + 1 topic-specific
- Recommended set for Adam:
  - Broad: `#mortgage` `#realestate`
  - Niche: `#mortgageloanoffice` `#loanofficer` `#firsttimehomebuyer`
  - Local: `#austintx` `#austinrealestate` `#austinhomes`
  - Topic: `#homebuying` `#mortgagetips` `#ratewatch`
- Source: [Sprout Social LinkedIn Hashtags 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/linkedin-hashtags/)

**Posting Window:**
- Weekdays 7am–4pm CT
- Peak: Wednesday 4pm CT
- Minimum cadence for algorithm consideration: 3x/week

### Instagram

**Hashtag Strategy (Post-December 2024):**
- **3–5 hashtags per post** — not 20–30 anymore
- Priority: **keyword-rich captions over hashtag density**
- Include relevant keywords naturally in caption text (e.g., "Austin mortgage rates", "first-time homebuyer Texas")
- Hashtag sets:
  - Local: `#austintx` `#austinrealestate` `#atxhomes`
  - Niche: `#mortgagetips` `#homebuying101` `#firsthome`
  - Branded (optional, build over time): `#AdamStyerMortgage`
- Source: [Hootsuite Instagram Hashtags 2025](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-hashtags/) | [Later Instagram Hashtag Strategy](https://later.com/resources/guide/instagram-hashtag-strategy/)

**Content Format Notes:**
- Reels for discovery (reach non-followers)
- Carousels for engagement (higher engagement rate per person)
- Keywords in captions drive searchability more than hashtags post-Dec 2024

**Posting Window:**
- 6pm–9pm weekdays CT
- Peak: Thursday 9am CT

### Facebook

**Reality Check:**
- Organic reach: 1–2% (not worth optimizing for)
- Reels + Groups are the only viable organic plays
- Austin-focused Facebook Group strategy is the long-term play (see Open Questions)

## Austin TX Market Data Sources

These are the authoritative sources for weekly local market data — ideal for Adam's "Austin Market Update" content pillar:

**Primary:**
1. **Unlock MLS** — [unlockmls.com/stats](https://www.unlockmls.com/stats)
   - Most complete, accurate housing data in the Austin region
   - Covers Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos MSA, individual counties (Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, Caldwell)
   - Free, public access
   - Updated weekly

2. **Team Price Real Estate** — [teamprice.com/market-update](https://teamprice.com/market-update)
   - Residential reports updated weekly
   - Good narrative framing of raw stats — useful for social post inspiration
   - Also publishes "Week in Review" posts every Friday

3. **KXAN Austin Housing Data** — [kxan.com/news/housing/austin-housing-market-data/](https://www.kxan.com/news/housing/austin-housing-market-data/)
   - Updated monthly from Unlock MLS + Zillow data
   - News anchor framing — easy to reference for post copy

**Current Austin Market Snapshot (March 2026):**
- Median sold price: $445,250 (down 2.4% YoY from $410,000 in Feb 2026 to $445,250 in Mar 2026)
- All-time peak was $550,000 in May 2022 — current pricing is ~19% below peak
- Active listings: 14,443 (6.6% more than this same point in 2025)
- Months of inventory: 6.5 (Austin metro) — buyer's market territory
- Bastrop County: most inventory at 10.1 months
- Williamson County: least inventory at 5.8 months

**How to use for weekly content:**
- Pull one metric from Unlock MLS or Team Price every week
- Frame it for homebuyers: "More homes on the market = more negotiating power for buyers"
- Frame it for realtors on LinkedIn: "Buyer's market = close more deals by pre-approving before listing tours"

## Compliance Requirements (Additions to Week 1 Baseline)

**ActiveComply additions:**
- Regulators treat Instagram and all online content as **marketing communications** — all compliance rules apply equally to social media
- Loan officers must use **professional pages** (not personal profiles) for any product advertising or self-promotion
- Live videos and Stories carry the same compliance requirements as static posts — NMLS# and disclaimers required
- Co-marketing content on social must be distributed to the general public — cannot target specific consumers you have a pre-existing relationship with

## Content Pillar Framework — Draft

Based on all Week 1 research, the following 5 pillars are recommended for Adam's content system. This is a **draft** — finalize after Adam's account audit reveals what's already working.

### Pillar 1: Rate Education
- What: Weekly rate watch, what's moving rates, when to lock vs. float
- Why: Positions Adam as a knowledgeable expert; high search intent
- NMLS# 513013 required on all posts. APR disclosure if specific rate mentioned.
- Formats: LinkedIn carousel (rate trend breakdown), Instagram Reel (30-sec "rates this week")
- Frequency: 1x/week per platform
- CTA: "DM me for a free rate quote" or "Check your buying power → link in bio"

### Pillar 2: Austin Market Data
- What: Weekly local housing stats — inventory, median price, days on market, county breakdown
- Why: Differentiates Adam as Austin-specific expert; realtors and buyers want local data
- Data source: Unlock MLS (weekly), Team Price weekly brief
- Formats: LinkedIn carousel (data visualization + what it means), Instagram carousel (simplified stat + takeaway)
- Frequency: 1x/week per platform (Friday)
- CTA: "Thinking of buying in Austin? Let's run the numbers → [application link]"

### Pillar 3: Buyer Education
- What: Home buying process, down payment myths, credit scores, loan types explained simply
- Why: Builds trust with first-time buyers; evergreen content with long shelf life
- Formats: LinkedIn carousel (step-by-step), Instagram Reel (myth vs. fact)
- Frequency: 1x/week per platform
- CTA: "Apply now: mslp.my1003app.com/513013/register"

### Pillar 4: Realtor Resources
- What: Tools, tips, and insights for realtor partners — contract timelines, pre-approval best practices, how to close faster
- Why: Adam's primary referral source is realtors — this content attracts and retains referral partners
- Primary platform: LinkedIn (B2B audience)
- Formats: LinkedIn carousel ("What every Austin realtor should know about pre-approvals this quarter")
- Frequency: 1x/week (LinkedIn only)
- CTA: "Send your buyers to get pre-approved before their first showing → forward this"

### Pillar 5: Personal Brand
- What: Austin community content, behind-the-scenes as a LO, personal stories, faith-driven authenticity
- Why: Humans buy from humans — the authenticity signal is the differentiator
- Formats: Phone-shot video (unpolished), text-only LinkedIn post, Instagram Story
- Frequency: 1x/week per platform
- Note: No compliance issues typically unless discussing specific rates. Still include NMLS# in bio/profile.

## Recommended Approach

1. **LinkedIn is the highest-priority platform** — B2B (realtor referrals) + best ROI for LOs. Build carousel content first.
2. **Carousel PDF format** is the primary format for LinkedIn. Target 12 slides, visual + brief bullets, value-first structure.
3. **Instagram** = repurpose LinkedIn carousels as simplified visual posts + original Reels. Keywords in captions > hashtag volume.
4. **Facebook** = cross-post from Instagram. Not worth building independent content for until organic reach improves or Group strategy is launched.
5. **Weekly rhythm** = Monday (Pillar 1 — Rate), Friday (Pillar 2 — Austin Market), Wednesday (rotate Pillars 3/4/5)
