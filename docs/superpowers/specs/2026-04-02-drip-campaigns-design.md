# Drip Campaigns System — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-04-02
**Status:** Approved
**Approach:** Hybrid — Template Skeletons + Claude Polish

## Overview

A drip campaign system for LoanOS that automatically sends personalized emails and handwritten cards to past clients, cold leads, and realtor partners. Emails are generated by Claude from short skeleton prompts combined with real borrower/loan data. High-stakes emails require Adam's approval; low-stakes emails auto-send with a daily digest.

Pipeline engagement emails are excluded — Arive handles those already.

## Design Principles

- Every email earns its right to exist (value-first + personalized to real data)
- Voice varies by audience: knowledgeable friend (past clients), straight shooter (leads), quiet confidence (realtors)
- Smart auto-enrollment from pipeline events
- Smart auto-removal when status changes (lead → under contract = stop nurture)
- Manual add/remove override always available
- Weekly newsletter continues separately as broadcast — drips layer on top
- Handwritten card integration for high-touch moments
- 14-day frequency guardrail prevents pile-ups across campaigns

## 1. Campaign Definitions

### Campaign 1: Past Client Retention

**Audience:** Closed borrowers
**Cadence:** 4-6 touchpoints/year
**Tone:** `knowledgeable_friend`

| # | Touchpoint | Timing | Approval | Channel |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Closing Anniversary | Yearly on close date | Auto-send | Email + Handwritten card |
| 2 | Birthday | Yearly on birthday | Auto-send | Email + Handwritten card |
| 3 | Rate Drop Alert | Current rate ≥ 0.75% below their locked rate | Needs approval | Email |
| 4 | Equity Check-In | 6 months post-close, then yearly | Auto-send | Email |
| 5 | Seasonal Value Touch | 2x/year (March + October) | Auto-send | Email |
| 6 | Holiday | Thanksgiving + New Year | Auto-send | Email |

**Skeleton examples:**
- **Closing Anniversary:** "Congrats on [X] year(s) in [address]. Mention equity change. Mention rate environment if relevant. Light — no hard sell."
- **Rate Drop Alert:** "Their rate: [X]. Current: [Y]. Monthly savings. 5-year savings. Direct CTA."
- **Equity Check-In:** "Estimated value vs purchase price. Equity gained. Mention HELOC/cash-out if substantial. Educational, not salesy."
- **Birthday:** "Happy birthday. Keep it warm and personal. No business talk."
- **Seasonal Value Touch:** "Homestead exemption reminder (spring) or year-end tax tip (fall). Timely, useful, local."
- **Holiday:** "Warm, personal. Reference their home. Gratitude, not marketing."

### Campaign 2: Lead Nurture

Three sub-campaigns based on lead temperature.

**Sub-campaign 2A: Ghost Referral** (realtor sent them, they went quiet)
**Tone:** `straight_shooter`

| # | Touchpoint | Timing | Approval |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Warm intro follow-up | 3 days after initial outreach | Auto-send |
| 2 | Value add | 7 days — market snapshot or affordability insight | Auto-send |
| 3 | Soft check-in | 21 days | Auto-send |
| 4 | Final touch | 45 days — "door's always open" | Auto-send |
| — | Move to long-term nurture | After day 45 | — |

**Sub-campaign 2B: Incomplete Application** (started app, never finished)
**Tone:** `straight_shooter`

| # | Touchpoint | Timing | Approval |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Helpful nudge | 2 days — "saw you started, here's what to expect" | Auto-send |
| 2 | Common questions | 5 days — address typical hesitations | Auto-send |
| 3 | Personal offer to help | 14 days — "want to jump on a quick call?" | Auto-send |

**Sub-campaign 2C: Went Quiet After Conversation** (had real contact, timing wasn't right)
**Tone:** `straight_shooter`

| # | Touchpoint | Timing | Approval |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Market update relevant to their situation | 30 days | Auto-send |
| 2 | Rate/affordability change | 60 days | Auto-send |
| 3 | Check-in | 90 days | Auto-send |
| 4 | Quarterly touch | Every 90 days ongoing | Auto-send |
| — | Move to long-term nurture after 1 year | | — |

**Long-term nurture** (all cold leads land here eventually): Newsletter only + 1-2 seasonal emails/year. If they re-engage (open, click, reply), they re-enter an active sequence.

### Campaign 3: Realtor Relationships

**Audience:** Active referral partners
**Cadence:** 3-4 touchpoints/year
**Tone:** `quiet_confidence`

| # | Touchpoint | Timing | Approval |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Deal anniversary | 1 year since first closed loan together | Auto-send |
| 2 | Milestone celebration | Every 5th loan closed together (5, 10, 15...) | Needs approval |
| 3 | Co-marketing offer | 2x/year — "want me to make you an open house flyer?" | Needs approval |
| 4 | Holiday | 1x/year — Thanksgiving or New Year | Auto-send |

## 3. Enrollment & Exit Rules

### Auto-Enrollment Triggers

| Event | Campaign | How |
|-------|----------|-----|
| Loan status → "closed" in Arive | Past Client Retention | Arive Status Update workflow triggers enrollment |
| Realtor referral comes in, no app after 3 days | Ghost Referral | New Application Received workflow checks for app status |
| Website form fill, no app after 2 days | Incomplete Application | Web Lead Automation workflow checks for app status |
| Contact had conversation, marked "warm" but no app | Went Quiet | Manual tag or Salesforce stage triggers it |
| First loan closed with a realtor | Realtor Relationship | Arive Status Update detects first close with that agent |

### Auto-Exit Rules

| Rule | Campaigns Affected | Logic |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| Contact status → `under_contract` or `active_loan` | All lead nurture | Remove immediately, reason: "entered pipeline" |
| Contact status → `closed` | Lead nurture → enroll in Past Client | Transition, not just removal |
| Contact unsubscribed | All | Remove from all, never re-enroll automatically |
| Email bounced 2+ times | All | Remove, flag contact |
| Enrollment completed all steps | Sequential campaigns | Status → "completed", move to long-term nurture if applicable |
| Contact marked inactive/dead | All | Remove immediately |

### Manual Overrides (always available in LoanOS UI)

- Add any contact to any campaign
- Remove any contact from any campaign with a reason
- Pause/resume an individual enrollment
- Skip a specific step for a specific person

## 4. Dashboard UI

Located at `/dashboard/drip-campaigns`. Three levels of depth.

### Level 1: Campaign Overview

- All campaigns listed with status, step count, enrollment count, last send date
- Top stats: total active enrollments, sent this month, awaiting approval
- "Approval Queue" button with badge count for pending emails
- Click any campaign row to drill into detail

### Level 2: Campaign Detail

Four tabs per campaign:

**Steps & Skeletons** — Every touchpoint with trigger type, timing, channel, approval setting, skeleton text. Editable inline.

**Enrolled Contacts** — Searchable, filterable table showing: contact name, property, enrollment date, next send date, next step. Row actions: Pause, Remove, Skip Next Step, View History.

**Send History** — Log of every email/card sent from this campaign. Shows: contact, step, date, status (sent/queued/skipped), link to view generated email.

**Exit Rules** — Visual display of active rules with ability to edit thresholds (e.g., change rate drop threshold from 0.75% to 1.0%).

### Level 3: Approval Queue

Separate view accessible from the top nav badge. Shows all pending emails across all campaigns:
- Generated email preview (subject + body)
- Who it's for, which campaign/step triggered it
- Contact context (property, loan details)
- Actions: Approve, Edit & Approve, Skip, Remove from Campaign

## 5. Email Generation Flow

Daily scheduler runs via n8n (upgrade existing Drip Email Scheduler, workflow ID: `LqBb3YDLjS2eUrDE`) at 7am CT.

```
Daily scheduler
  │
  ├─ Query drip_enrollments WHERE status = 'active' AND next_send_at <= now()
  │
  ├─ For each enrollment:
  │   ├─ Check exit rules first → if triggered, remove and skip
  │   ├─ Check 14-day frequency guardrail → if recent send, defer
  │   ├─ Pull contact data from Supabase (name, email, property, loan details)
  │   ├─ Pull step skeleton from drip_steps
  │   ├─ Pull real market data (current rates, home value estimate if available)
  │   │
  │   ├─ Send to Claude API (model: claude-sonnet-4-5):
  │   │   - Skeleton prompt
  │   │   - Contact/loan data
  │   │   - Tone setting (from step config)
  │   │   - System prompt: "Write as Adam Styer. Short punchy sentences. Conversational."
  │   │
  │   ├─ Claude returns: subject + body
  │   │
  │   ├─ If requires_approval = true:
  │   │   └─ Insert into drip_sends (status: 'queued') → Approval Queue
  │   │
  │   ├─ If requires_approval = false:
  │   │   └─ Insert into drip_sends (status: 'sent') → Send via Outlook
  │   │
  │   ├─ If channel = 'both' or 'handwritten_card':
  │   │   └─ Queue handwritten.com API order (cancelable 24hrs)
  │   │
  │   └─ Update enrollment: current_step++, calculate next_send_at
  │
  └─ Log run to automation_runs table
```

### Tone Configuration

| Audience | Tone | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Past clients | `knowledgeable_friend` | Warm, substantive, references their specific situation |
| Leads | `straight_shooter` | Direct, no fluff, clear value and CTA |
| Realtors | `quiet_confidence` | Professional, peer-level, focused on partnership |

### Frequency Guardrail

Before sending, the scheduler checks: "Has this contact received ANY drip email in the last 14 days?" If yes, defer the send to next_send_at + 14 days. Prevents pile-ups when someone has overlapping triggers (birthday + anniversary same month) or is in multiple campaigns.

## 6. Handwritten Card Integration

**Service:** Handwritten.com (or similar with API access — confirm during implementation)

### Flow

1. Drip step triggers with `channel: 'both'` or `'handwritten_card'`
2. Claude generates a shorter, warmer version of the message for the card
3. System creates card order via API: recipient name + address, message text, card style
4. Order appears in "Pending Cards" section of dashboard — cancelable for 24 hours
5. After 24 hours → order confirmed → card ships

### Card Triggers

- Closing anniversary (year 1, 2, etc.)
- Birthday
- Optionally: milestone celebration with realtors (5th, 10th loan together)

## 7. Architecture Summary

| Component | Where | What |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Campaign config | Supabase (`drip_campaigns`, `drip_steps`) | Campaign definitions, skeletons, rules |
| Enrollment engine | n8n workflows (existing + new triggers) | Auto-enroll/exit based on pipeline events |
| Daily scheduler | n8n (upgraded `LqBb3YDLjS2eUrDE`) | Generate emails, send or queue daily |
| Email generation | Claude API via n8n | Personalized emails from skeletons + data |
| Email delivery | Outlook via n8n | Sends from Adam's real email address |
| Card orders | Handwritten.com API via n8n | Queue and confirm handwritten card orders |
| Dashboard UI | LoanOS Next.js (`/dashboard/drip-campaigns`) | Manage campaigns, enrollments, approval queue |
| Approval queue | LoanOS UI + `drip_sends` table | Review/approve/edit high-stakes emails |

## 8. What This Does NOT Include

- **Pipeline engagement emails** — Arive handles these already
- **Newsletter** — Continues as a separate manual broadcast system
- **Email open/click tracking** — Not in v1. Can add later via Outlook read receipts or a tracking pixel service.
- **A/B testing** — Not in v1. Skeleton approach makes this easy to add later.
- **Unsubscribe management UI** — v1 uses manual removal. Proper unsubscribe link + management page is a fast follow.
