## Section 2 — The Problem We Solve

Most loan officers are patching together 5–7 different tools that don't talk to each other. Here's what that looks like:

**The typical LO's stack (and its problems):**
| Tool | What It Does | What's Wrong With It |
|------|-------------|---------------------|

**The real cost:**
- $400–600/month in software
- 5–10 hours/week on manual follow-up and data entry
- Deals that fall through because follow-up was late or inconsistent
- No AI in the workflow — everything is manual

**What LoanOS does:**
Replaces that entire stack with one platform. Contacts, pipeline, automation, AI-written emails, scenario calculators, daily briefings — all connected, all working automatically.

## Section 3 — Who Buys This

**Primary target: Independent mortgage brokers and small LO teams**

Best fit profile:
- 1–3 person operation (solo LO or LO + processor)
- Closing 20–60+ loans per year
- Currently using Jungo, BNTouch, or a cobbled-together Salesforce setup
- Has tried Zapier or other automation tools and found them too fragile
- Wants to scale without hiring another person
- Tech-comfortable but not a developer

**Secondary target: Small mortgage companies (2–10 LOs)**
- Team leads who want to standardize communication across LOs
- Operations managers who want pipeline visibility without manual reporting

**Who is NOT a fit (save everyone's time):**
- Large banks / retail lenders (they have enterprise software)
- LOs who are not willing to adopt new tools
- LOs under 15 loans/year (not enough volume to justify the automation ROI)

## Section 4 — Feature Walkthrough

Walk through these features in this order during a demo.

### 4.1 — Dashboard
The command center. At a glance: pipeline loans, gross commission, commission YTD, this month's closings. Stage breakdown cards. "Needs Attention" section — loans with no activity in 3+ days. Everything links to filtered views.

**What to say:** "This replaces the Monday morning spreadsheet review. Every number is live."

### 4.2 — Daily Briefing (AI Agent)
Claude AI analyzes the full pipeline every morning and generates 7 prioritized action items. "Call Scott Tillman — rate lock expires in 2 days." "Maria Gutierrez hasn't had activity in 8 days." No more forgetting who to call.

**What to say:** "This is like having a Chief of Staff who read your entire pipeline before you got to the office."

### 4.3 — Contacts Module
Full CRM with 2,400+ contacts imported from Salesforce. Smart Lists (Leads, Pre-Approved, In Process, Realtor Partners). Inline stage editing. Drag-and-drop column reorder. Bulk actions. AI Outreach Chat — select 10 leads, type "write a check-in email for leads who haven't heard from me in 30 days," get 10 personalized emails.

**What to say:** "Everything Jungo does, without the Salesforce tax."

### 4.4 — Loan Pipeline
816+ historical loans imported. Filterable by stage, loan type, date range. Loan detail page with inline editing, rate lock countdown, milestone timeline, commission tracking, and a full activity log. Arive webhook integration — when a loan updates in Arive, LoanOS knows automatically.

**What to say:** "Your pipeline in Arive stays in sync with LoanOS. One source of truth."

### 4.5 — Automations
One-click triggers for the most time-consuming communication tasks:
- **Pre-Approval Email** — upload the PA letter PDF, LoanOS extracts the loan details and writes the email
- **Referral Intro Email** — paste referral info, get a personalized intro email drafted in Outlook
- **Final CD Email** — upload the CD PDF, email drafted automatically
- **Loan Milestone Notifications** — when Arive fires a milestone (approved, CTC, closing), LoanOS writes borrower AND realtor emails simultaneously
- **Post-Closing Review Request** — automated 48-hour follow-up asking for a Google/Zillow review

**What to say:** "Every email in this list used to take 10–15 minutes to write. Now it takes 30 seconds to review and send."

### 4.6 — Calculator Suite (Phase 3 — in progress)
- **Loan Scenario Comparator** — side-by-side loan options with AI narrative explanation
- **Refi Analyzer** — break-even analysis with plain-English summary
- Coming: Rent vs. Buy, Total Cost of Homeownership, Buy Now vs. Wait

**What to say:** "This is what you used to pay Mortgage Coach $100/month for — and ours actually explains the math in plain English, written by AI for each specific borrower."

### 4.7 — AI Chat (per record)
Every contact and every loan has a floating AI chat panel. "What's the status of this loan?" "Draft a follow-up email to this borrower." The AI has full context — the contact's info, their loan, their stage, their activity history.

**What to say:** "It's like having a junior LO assistant who has read every note in your CRM."

## Section 7 — Demo Script

**Duration:** 20–30 minutes

**Before the demo:**
- Ask what tools they're currently using and what frustrates them most
- Ask how many loans they close per year and how big their team is
- Get access to a screenshare

**Demo flow:**

1. **(2 min) The problem** — "How long does it take you to write a pre-approval email after issuing a PA? What about a realtor update when a loan hits CTC?" Let them answer. Then: "I'm going to show you what that looks like in LoanOS."

2. **(3 min) Dashboard** — Show the pipeline overview. Point out Needs Attention. Click a KPI card to show the filtered view. "This is your Monday morning review, automated."

3. **(5 min) Automations** — This is the demo centerpiece. Show the Pre-Approval Email trigger — upload a PA PDF, watch it extract the loan details and draft the email. "That took 25 seconds."

4. **(5 min) AI Chat + Daily Briefing** — Open a loan record. Show the chat panel. Type "draft a follow-up email to this borrower." Show the Daily Briefing page — 7 prioritized action items.

5. **(5 min) Contacts + Pipeline** — Show Smart Lists, inline editing, the Arive sync. "Every time a loan updates in Arive, LoanOS already knows."

6. **(3 min) Calculator** — Show the Scenario Comparator. Run two quick scenarios. Show the AI-generated narrative.

7. **(5 min) Q&A + close** — "What would be most valuable to your workflow? What would you want to use first?"

**Close:** "We're onboarding a small group of LOs in Phase 4. If this is a fit, I want to make sure you have a spot. What would you need to see to move forward?"

## Section 8 — Mortgage Industry Glossary

Know these terms. Using the wrong vocabulary kills credibility with LOs.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| LO | Loan Officer |
| LOS | Loan Origination System (e.g., Arive, Encompass) |
| PA / Pre-Approval | Letter stating a borrower is approved up to a certain amount |
| CD | Closing Disclosure — final loan cost breakdown, sent before closing |
| CTC | Clear to Close — all conditions met, ready to fund |
| UW | Underwriting / Underwriter |
| PTD | Prior to Docs — conditions needed before closing docs drawn |
| PTF | Prior to Funding — conditions needed before wire goes out |
| Rate lock | Guaranteed interest rate for a fixed period |
| EMD | Earnest Money Deposit |
| DTI | Debt-to-Income ratio |
| LTV | Loan-to-Value ratio |
| PMI | Private Mortgage Insurance (conventional) |
| MIP | Mortgage Insurance Premium (FHA) |
| DU / LP | Desktop Underwriter (Fannie Mae) / Loan Prospector (Freddie Mac) — automated underwriting systems |
| 1003 | Uniform Residential Loan Application |
| TC | Transaction Coordinator |
| TREC | Texas Real Estate Commission (contract forms) |
