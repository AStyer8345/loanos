## Source 1: Mortgage Lead Conversion Benchmarks (HousingWire)
URL: https://www.housingwire.com/articles/mortgage-lead-generation/
- Industry baseline: 1–3% of purchased leads convert within 120 days
- Increasing form conversion from 2% → 3% = 10 extra leads/month per 1,000 visitors

- Referral traffic converts highest because trust is pre-built

## Source 2: FCC Lead Gen Loophole — TCPA Impact on Mortgage (HousingWire)
URL: https://www.housingwire.com/articles/how-new-fcc-rules-closing-the-lead-gen-loophole-could-impact-mortgage-professionals/
- New FCC order: "express consent" rules now require ONE-TO-ONE consent — bundled consent forms are illegal
- Effective 2026: Gathering consumer data and selling to multiple lenders without 1:1 consent = illegal
- **Direct relevance to BLOCKER-001** — the /get-preapproved form must name Adam Styer specifically in consent language
- Carve-outs: existing borrower relationships and explicit written authorization still allowed
- New FCRA amendment (March 5, 2026): trigger leads now restricted — outreach only via consent or existing relationship

## Source 3: TCPA Litigation Risk Rising for Mortgage Lenders (National Mortgage News)
URL: https://www.nationalmortgagenews.com/opinion/the-risk-of-tcpa-litigation-for-mortgage-lenders-is-increasing
- TCPA class action suits hitting mortgage lenders more frequently
- Lenders must document proof of consent in real-time — not just checkbox
- Recommended: timestamp + IP + exact disclosure text logged at form submission
- Bundled consent (form sends lead to multiple lenders) = highest litigation risk

## Source 4: Email Nurture Campaign Guide (Mailchimp)
URL: https://mailchimp.com/resources/nurture-campaign/
- Businesses using marketing automation in nurture see 451% increase in qualified leads
- Behavioral triggers (email open, link click) are most effective — respond at peak engagement
- Sequence logic: each email must build on the previous or add new information
- Mobile-friendly + concise subject lines + one clear CTA per email
- Relevant content per segment beats generic broadcasts every time
