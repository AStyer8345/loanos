# Web Research — GSC Monitoring + Content Indexation — 2026-04-05

## Topic: Google Search Console Monitoring — Impressions & Clicks

**Source:** https://searchengineland.com/why-google-search-console-impressions-dropped-interpret-data-463677
**Title:** Why GSC Impressions Dropped — How to Interpret Search Console Data (Search Engine Land)
**Summary:** Explains how to read impression and click trends in Search Console, flagging that GSC now includes AI Mode traffic data which can cause fluctuations in impression counts that don't reflect organic ranking changes. Key metrics to monitor per page: impressions (visibility), clicks (traffic), CTR (content appeal), and avg. position (ranking trend). The Insights report in GSC gives a 3-month trend view that is the right starting point for a first post-index monitoring window.

## Topic: GSC URL Inspection + First 30 Days of New Content

**Source:** https://searchengineland.com/guide/google-search-console-guide
**Title:** The Complete Guide to Google Search Console (Search Engine Land)
**Summary:** New mortgage broker content typically takes a few days to 2–3 weeks to crawl and index depending on domain authority and content frequency; URL Inspection tool lets you request immediate indexing for high-priority pages. The Index Status report confirms when pages are indexed vs. discovered-not-indexed vs. excluded, which is critical for monitoring the 10+ blog posts published in the March–April 2026 batch. GSC filters by date range, device, and country allow isolation of post-publication traffic vs. pre-existing baseline — essential for measuring new content ROI.
