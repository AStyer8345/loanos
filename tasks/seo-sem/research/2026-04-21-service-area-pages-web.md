Date: 2026-04-21

## Summary
Service area pages should link to relevant local business listings and create seamless user experience connecting visitors with geo-targeted content. Local SEO CTR is driven by reviews — studies show up to 25% CTR increase when a Google Business Profile moves from 3-star to 5-star average. Geo-targeted link building (local directories, local blogs) enhances location page authority.
