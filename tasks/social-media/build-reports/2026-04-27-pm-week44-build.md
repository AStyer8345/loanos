### Post 190 — LinkedIn Real Talk → authority — Wed Jan 6 9 AM CT (15:00 UTC)
```
If a realtor sends you a buyer with a pre-approval letter from a lender you've never heard of, that's data.

Not bad data. Not good data. Just data.

Half the time it's a lender with a great rate engine and a thin team — closes get bumpy in week three.

The other half it's a relationship the buyer trusts. That one's harder to compete with, and worth respecting.

Your move isn't to badmouth the other lender. It's to ask better questions: How fast did they pull credit? Have they reviewed the bank statements? Have they talked DTI?

Most pre-approval letters are paper. The work behind them is what matters.

If the work isn't done, the buyer finds out in week three of escrow.
```
