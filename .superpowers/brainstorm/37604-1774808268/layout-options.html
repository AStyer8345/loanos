Social Media Dashboard — Layout

This will live as a new tab inside your Marketing page, next to SEND, CALLS, and HISTORY

A

List + Side Panel

Left side: scrollable list of all drafts (newest first) with status badges (Draft, Approved, Scheduled, Posted). Click a draft → right side opens a detail panel with preview, inline editor, and Claude chat. Similar to an email client layout.

└──────────────┴─────────────────────────────┘
B

Calendar View

Monthly calendar grid showing scheduled slots. Click a day → see the posts for that day. Good for seeing the big picture of what's planned, but harder to quickly scan/edit individual posts.

          Click a cell to open post details
C

Kanban Board

Columns for each status: Draft → In Review → Approved → Scheduled → Posted. Drag posts between columns. Visual workflow, but takes more screen space and less info density than the list view.

┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
│ DRAFT │ REVIEW │ APPROVED │ SCHEDULED│
├──────────┼──────────┼──────────┼──────────┤

│ Post #11 │ │ Post #7 │ Post #4 │
│ │ │ Post #6 │ │

My recommendation: Option A (List + Side Panel). It's the fastest for reviewing and editing posts one by one — which is your primary workflow. Calendar can be a secondary view toggle later. Kanban looks nice but adds friction for a solo operator.
