## Sources Added to NotebookLM This Session

### 1. Unbounce — Average Landing Page Conversion Rates (Q4 2024)
URL: https://unbounce.com/average-conversion-rates-landing-pages/
**Key findings:**
- 83% of landing page visits happen on mobile — mobile-first design is critical
- Short mortgage/real estate landing pages convert at ~33% higher rates than long-form pages
- Moving conversion rate from 2% to 3% on 1,000 monthly visitors = 120 additional leads/year
- Industry average conversion rate varies widely; financial services typically 4-8%

### 2. Mailchimp — Landing Page Best Practices for High Conversion Rates
URL: https://mailchimp.com/resources/landing-page-best-practices/
**Key findings:**
- Single-purpose pages dramatically outperform multi-purpose pages — remove navigation
- Social proof (testimonials, review counts) is the #1 trust signal for financial services
- Form length: 3 fields max for top-of-funnel capture; ask for more only after initial opt-in
- Headline clarity: visitor should know the offer within 3 seconds of landing

### 3. Scotsman Guide — Flip the Script on Mortgage Lead Generation
URL: https://www.scotsmanguide.com/residential/flip-the-script-on-mortgagelead-generation/
**Key findings:**
- Most LOs wait for referrals; proactive outbound digital lead gen is the competitive differentiator
- Content marketing (rate commentary, educational guides) builds top-of-funnel before intent forms
- Email nurture sequences with 6-8 touches over 90 days significantly outperform single-touch follow-up
- Past client reactivation campaigns have higher ROI than any cold lead acquisition channel

### 4. Scotsman Guide — Lenders Increasingly Turn to AI and Automation (2026 Survey)
URL: https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/lenders-increasingly-turn-to-technology-and-automation/
**Key findings:**
- 63% of brokers excited about AI for lead qualification and prospecting
- Automation ROI: lenders using workflow automation report 30-40% reduction in time-per-loan
- Agentic AI for lead routing and follow-up is the #1 technology investment priority in 2026
- Brokers who automate lead capture + nurture sequences outperform non-automated peers by 2-3x in conversion
