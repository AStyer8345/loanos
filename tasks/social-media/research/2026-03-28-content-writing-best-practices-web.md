## Source 1 — Instagram Hashtag Strategy 2026

**URL:** https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/what-clickable-reels-links-and-hashtag-limits-mean-for-your-2026-instagram-strategy/
**Source:** Social Media Examiner (authoritative)

**Summary:** Instagram has reduced the hashtag limit to 5 per post in 2026, with some users restricted to just 3. Instagram's AI now understands content context without relying on hashtag volume, meaning keyword-rich captions are more effective for discovery than hashtag stacking. For mortgage professionals, the priority shift is clear: invest in descriptive, keyword-forward caption copy rather than loading up on tags.

## Source 2 — LinkedIn Carousel Best Practices 2026

**URL:** https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-carousels/
**Source:** Buffer (authoritative)

**Summary:** LinkedIn document/PDF carousels continue to dramatically outperform other post types — 278% more engagement than video, 303% more than image posts, and 596% more than text-only posts, based on analysis of over 1 million LinkedIn posts. Best practices for 2026: lead with a hook slide, keep slide text short and scannable, place the CTA on the final slide to reward swipe-through behavior, and front-load the caption with the key hook since LinkedIn truncates after ~100 characters.

## Source 3 — Publer Draft Posts & Scheduling Documentation

**URL:** https://help.publer.io/en/article/how-to-create-draft-posts-1h1g1o0/
**Source:** Publer Help Center (official)

**Summary:** Publer supports draft posts via the Create composer — click "Draft" instead of schedule to save undated drafts under the Posts > Drafts tab. Dated drafts appear in the Calendar view. The Publer API uses workspace and account IDs to target platform destinations; the agent environment could not reach the API endpoint directly this session (curl commands documented in QA report for manual execution). Publer supports 4 scheduling modes: manual, AutoSchedule, recurring, and recycling.
