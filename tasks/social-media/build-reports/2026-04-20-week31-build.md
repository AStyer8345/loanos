> This trips up first-time buyers constantly. They see a number, assume it's locked. It's not. That quote floats with the market until you actually lock it.

**Classification:** EVERGREEN
**Final copy:**
> Rates dropped. The market didn't open. Here's why.
>
> Homeowners who bought or refinanced in 2020 and 2021 got historically low rates. Those people are not moving. Selling means trading that mortgage for one that costs hundreds more per month. That math doesn't work for most people.
>
> That's not an Austin problem. That's a structural problem — and it's keeping inventory low even as rates have come down from their peak.
>
> The market "opening up" would require either rates returning to 2021 levels — which nobody's predicting — or enough of those homeowners deciding to move anyway. Neither is happening fast.
>
> Buyers waiting for inventory to normalize might be waiting a long time.
>
> Better move: Get your numbers dialed in now. When the right house shows up, you don't want to still be figuring out your budget.
>
