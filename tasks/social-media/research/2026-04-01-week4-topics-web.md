## VA Loan Content Angles

### Platform Fit
- **LinkedIn:** Professional angle — frame VA loans as an underutilized competitive advantage for Realtors working with veteran buyers. Carousel format works well (myths debunked slide-by-slide). Realtors who understand VA loans close more deals.
- **Instagram:** Veteran/buyer-facing. Personal storytelling hook — "Most veterans don't know this about their benefit." Reels (60–90 sec) or single-image carousel. DM-shareable: "Tag a veteran who needs to see this."
- **Facebook:** Broad audience; community tone. Good for a simple myth-busting post or a "Did you know?" format. No external links in caption body.

### Best Formats
- LinkedIn: 10–12 slide PDF carousel (myths → reality structure)
- Instagram: Reel with on-screen text or 3–5 image carousel; hook in first 3 seconds critical
- Facebook: Text-only or single image; focus on one myth per post for shareability

### Key Facts / Myths to Cover
1. **Myth: "$0 down means sellers won't accept my offer"** — Reality: In Austin's 2026 buyer's market, sellers need buyers. VA appraisals are not the obstacle they were in 2021.
2. **Myth: "VA loans are only for first-time buyers"** — Reality: No first-time buyer requirement. Reusable benefit.
3. **Myth: "VA loans are harder to close and take longer"** — Reality: Experienced LOs close VA loans at the same pace as conventional.
4. **Myth: "The funding fee makes it more expensive than conventional"** — Reality: No PMI. On a $400K loan, conventional PMI runs $100–$200/month. Over 30 years = up to $72,000. The 2.15% funding fee (first use, $0 down) = $8,600 one-time — often rolled in.
5. **Myth: "I can only use it once"** — Reality: Can be used multiple times. Funding fee is 3.3% on subsequent zero-down use (still no PMI).
6. **Key fact: Funding fee exemption** — Veterans with 10%+ VA disability rating pay $0 funding fee.
7. **Funding fee schedule (2026):** First use, 0% down = 2.15%; Subsequent use, 0% down = 3.3%; First use, 5% down = 1.5%; First use, 10%+ down = 1.25%

### Compliance Notes
- No specific APR disclosure required unless quoting a specific rate
- "Subject to credit approval" language required
- Equal Housing Lender logo on all visual posts
- NMLS #513013 on all posts
- Do NOT use "guaranteed approval" language
- If mentioning $0 down, confirm borrower must still meet eligibility requirements (service connection) — note this in copy

## Rate Buydown Content Angles

### Is This Still Relevant?
Yes — highly relevant in April 2026. Research confirms:
- Austin is in a buyer's market with 89–91 average days on market and 4.77–6.5 months of inventory
- Seller concessions of $5,000–$15,000 are standard on most Austin transactions in early 2026
- "Rate buydowns are one of the most underused tools in the current market and sellers are offering them left and right because concessions are standard in this buyer's market" (Neuhaus Realty Group)
- A $10,000 buydown saves $200+/month in years 1–2 vs. a $10,000 price cut saving only $53/month — this is a direct, shareable comparison

### Best Platform and Format
- **Primary: LinkedIn** — Realtor-facing educational content. Frame as a negotiation tool Realtors can use to get more offers accepted. Carousel or single data-visual.
- **Secondary: Instagram** — Buyer-facing. "Your seller can lower your payment for 2 years. Here's how." Reel or carousel.
- Facebook: low organic reach environment — use only if repurposing LinkedIn post; strip any external links

### Key Talking Points
1. **What it is:** A 2-1 buydown temporarily reduces the buyer's rate by 2% in year 1 and 1% in year 2, funded by the seller at closing
2. **The math (use real numbers):** On a $380,000 loan at 6.5%, a 2-1 buydown saves approximately $8,600 over two years; costs seller ~$9,000
3. **Price cut vs. buydown comparison:** $10,000 price cut = $53/month savings. $10,000 buydown = $200+/month savings in year 1. Buyers feel the buydown more.
4. **Realtor angle:** "Your listing isn't moving? A seller-funded buydown could generate the offer you need."
5. **Buyer angle:** "You're not stuck at today's rate for 30 years. Year 1 and Year 2 are lower."
6. **Note:** If rates drop significantly before the 2 years are up, buyer can refinance — the buydown still helped them get in the door

### Compliance Notes
- If quoting specific monthly payment amounts, include "Example only. Rate, terms, and payment amounts subject to change and credit approval."
- APR disclosure required only if quoting a specific interest rate
- NMLS #513013 required
- Equal Housing Lender on visual posts

## GBP Offer Post Research

### Format Requirements
Confirmed via Google's own support documentation:
- **Required fields:** Title (up to 58 characters), start date, end date/time
- **Optional fields:** Description (up to 1,500 characters), photo/video, coupon code, link, terms and conditions
- Google automatically suggests a 2-week expiration for new users but dates are configurable
- Post automatically generates a "View Offer" button — this is the highest CTA visibility of any GBP post type
- Offer posts remain live until the expiration date, unlike standard "What's New" posts which expire after 7 days

### Compliance Considerations
- Google restricts content that guarantees loan modification or foreclosure prevention — not applicable to standard purchase/rate offers
- For mortgage professionals, Google's financial services policies require verified profiles and restrict guaranteed-approval language
- Safe framing: Position the "offer" as a service/consultation offer (free rate quote, free pre-approval consultation, free buyer education session), NOT as a guaranteed rate
- If including a specific rate in the offer: APR disclosure required; include NMLS #513013; include Equal Housing Lender
- Safest compliant GBP Offer for a mortgage professional: "Free Pre-Approval Consultation — No Cost, No Obligation. Schedule in 2 minutes." — no rate quoted, no compliance landmines
- Alternative: "Down Payment Assistance Check — See if you qualify for $0 down or DPA programs in Austin." — educational/service framing

Include link to Calendly. NMLS #513013, Equal Housing Lender in terms field. This ties a timely market moment to a direct CTA and is the post type most likely to appear in map pack results.
