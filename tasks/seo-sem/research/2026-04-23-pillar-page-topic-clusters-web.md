# Web Research — Pillar Pages & Topic Clusters for Mortgage SEO
Date: 2026-04-23
Source: searchengineland.com/guide/topic-clusters
Topic: Pillar page strategy — relevant to "How to Buy a House in Austin TX 2026" pillar page (due Apr 24)

## Summary

Topic clusters are the modern SEO architecture for building topical authority: one comprehensive pillar page (the hub) links out to cluster pages covering subtopics, while all cluster pages link back to the pillar. Google rewards this structure because it signals depth and expertise on a given subject — critical for mortgage sites competing on competitive head terms like "mortgage broker Austin TX."

For styermortgage.com, the "How to Buy a House in Austin TX 2026" pillar page (targeting 3,000+ words) should link internally to all 24 suburb pages, all loan type pages (FHA, VA, Conventional, DSCR), and rate-check city pages. Each of those pages should already carry a backlink to the pillar — creating a closed cluster that reinforces topical authority for Austin homebuyer content.

The pillar page itself should be organized around questions buyers actually ask (H2 question format per our AEO strategy), cover the full buyer journey without going too deep on any one topic, and serve as the natural entry point that Google surfaces for high-volume, top-of-funnel queries.
