### June 17-18: FOMC Meeting
- 72% market probability of 25bp rate cut (4.50% → 4.25%) as of early April 2026
- This is the Fed rate cut most buyers are waiting for — critical educational opportunity
- KEY MISCONCEPTION: Most buyers think "Fed cuts = my mortgage rate drops"
  - Reality: Mortgage rates are tied to 10-yr Treasury / MBS market, NOT the Fed Funds Rate
  - The Fed Funds Rate affects HELOCs, auto loans, credit cards — NOT 30-yr fixed mortgages
  - Mortgage rates may actually RISE after a Fed cut if the market sells bonds (yield curve)
  - This is the single most common buyer misconception — correcting it builds major credibility
