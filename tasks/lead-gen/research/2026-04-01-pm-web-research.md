## Source 3 — New Trigger Lead Rules: A Lender's Guide
**URL**: https://www.nationalmortgagenews.com/news/new-trigger-lead-rules-a-lenders-guide
**Domain**: nationalmortgagenews.com (authoritative — mortgage trade)
**Relevance**: Compliance context — Homebuyers Privacy Protection Act (March 5, 2026)

**Summary**: With the Homebuyers Privacy Protection Act now in effect (March 5, 2026), lenders and brokers can no longer receive trigger leads from credit bureaus without explicit consumer consent. This makes owned-list lead generation via content marketing (resource guides, rate alerts, educational drips) the primary defensible strategy for 2026. This regulatory shift validates the entire lead gen program architecture — FTB guide as lead magnet is a direct response to the trigger lead ban.
