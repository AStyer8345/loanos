# Web Research — Email From Builder Feature
Date: 2026-03-29 PM
Topic: Tier 3 — Send scenario link to borrower via email directly from scenarios tab

## Key Findings

### 1. Engagement Tracking Is the Competitive Standard (Mortgage Maker)
Mortgage Maker shows LOs when borrowers view, edit, save, or order homeowners insurance on a presentation — enabling timely follow-up at the right moment. This is the bar to match or beat.
Source: https://mortgagemaker.ai/ + https://www.getapp.com/collaboration-software/a/mortgage-maker/

### 2. Digital Experience Is Now Baseline, Not Differentiator
- 90% of homebuyers want a more or fully digital process
- 58% more likely to choose a lender offering digital experience
- 68% would switch lenders for a better digital experience
A shareable scenario link sent via email IS part of this digital baseline expectation.
Source: https://mortgageworkspace.com/blog/how-to-build-a-fully-digital-scalable-mortgage-experience

### 3. LO Email Best Practice: Context + CTA
Empower LO's 2026 mortgage marketing guide emphasizes that every LO email should combine relevant context (market conditions, their specific situation) with a clear, non-pushy CTA. For a scenario email, this means: present the numbers briefly in the email body, then link to the full interactive view.
Source: https://empowerlo.com/blog/mortgage-marketing-guide-2026

### 4. Personalization Is Expected
AI-driven personalization — adapting messaging based on individual borrower behavior in real time — is now table stakes in fintech UX. The scenario email should auto-include the borrower's name, loan amount, and scenario type in the subject line and email body.
Source: https://www.bloomreach.com/en/blog/financial-services-email-marketing

## Design Implications for "Email From Builder"

1. **One-click send** from scenarios tab — pre-populated subject line and body
2. **Auto-include**: borrower name, scenario type (e.g., "Rent vs Own"), share link
3. **Tracking**: show LO when borrower opens the link (view event)
4. **Compliance**: no rate commitments in email body — link to scenario handles the numbers
5. **Mobile preview**: borrower will likely open on phone — subject line < 40 chars
