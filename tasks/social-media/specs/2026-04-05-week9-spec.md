**Beats:**

3. If your pre-approval is a real pre-approval — credit pulled, assets verified, income documented — you don't need 14 days. You need 5.
4. A seller sees two identical offers: 14-day contingency vs. 5-day. They take the 5. Every time.
5. Close: "If your pre-approval came with a printed letter but no actual underwriting — you don't have a real pre-approval. You have hope."
