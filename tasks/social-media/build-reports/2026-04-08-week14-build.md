**Post 85 — LinkedIn:**
PMI isn't permanent.

If you bought with less than 20% down, you're probably paying it right now. Here's when you can remove it.

Path 1 — You pay it down:
Under the Homeowners Protection Act (1998), lenders must automatically cancel PMI when you reach 22% equity based on original purchase price. You can request cancellation at 20%.

Path 2 — Your home appreciated:
Order an appraisal. If it shows you're at 80% LTV based on current value — submit it and request removal. This one is not automatic. You have to ask.

Two different paths. Two different processes. Most homeowners don't know either one exists.

If you've owned for 2+ years and you're still paying PMI — it's worth checking where you stand.

DM me PMI and I'll walk you through it.
