## Voice Framework Notes
- Post 50 (story): Specific details required — year, loan amount, what almost went wrong, what Adam learned. No mortgage pitch at the end. End on the lesson.
- Post 51 (Reel): Write how Adam talks at a BBQ — short sentences, pauses, direct. "Look. Here's the problem." Format: 0:00-0:03 hook / 0:03-0:40 body / 0:40-0:55 CTA
- Post 52 (carousel): Each slide = one idea. Not a listicle. Build to a conclusion. Slide 5 is the punchline.
- Post 53 (Facebook): More casual than LinkedIn version. Community feel. First-person, conversational.
- Post 54 (hot-take): <80 words. Strong stance. End with a punchline, not a CTA.
- Posts 55-56 (condo): Realtor-first voice. They are the audience. Talk peer-to-peer, not B2C.

## Platform Specs Applied
- LinkedIn: ≤150 words, ≤5 hashtags, no links in post body
- Instagram Reel: hook 0-3 seconds, total ≤60 seconds
- Instagram Static: 1080×1080, caption ≤150 words, 5-10 hashtags
- Facebook: ≤120 words, ≤3 hashtags
