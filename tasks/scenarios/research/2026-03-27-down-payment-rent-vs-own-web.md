## Key Takeaways

1. Break-even framing is standard in rent-vs-own: "After 5 years, buying saves $47K–$127K in 90% of US markets" — emotion-driving stat to use in narrative
2. Down payment comparisons need to show: monthly payment delta, total interest difference, PMI cost, equity position at N years
3. Best tools anchor on the decision outcome — "stay 5+ years? Buy beats rent" — not just numbers
4. Institutional tools (U.S. Bank, Navy Federal) use 4 core variables: rent, home price, down payment, years in home
5. Chart patterns: stacked area chart (principal vs interest), overlaid balance curves for equity comparison
