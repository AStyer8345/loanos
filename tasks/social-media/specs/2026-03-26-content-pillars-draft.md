## Overview

5 content pillars for Adam's social media system. Each pillar maps to a specific audience goal, platform priority, and content format. Together they deliver 5 posts/week across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook with zero overlap or redundancy.

**Weekly cadence:**
- Monday: Pillar 1 — Rate Education
- Wednesday: Pillar 3/4/5 (rotating)
- Friday: Pillar 2 — Austin Market Data
- + 2 additional posts/week from Pillars 3, 4, or 5 (Instagram/Facebook repurpose)

---

## Pillar 1: Rate Education

**Audience:** Buyers actively in market, clients comparison-shopping
**Goal:** Position Adam as trusted rate expert; drive DMs and applications
**Platforms:** LinkedIn (primary), Instagram (Reel), Facebook (cross-post)
**Frequency:** 1 LinkedIn post + 1 Instagram Reel per week = 2 posts

**Content types:**
- Weekly rate watch: "Rates this week — here's what moved them and what I'm watching"
- Rate myth-busting: "You don't need 20% down. Here's what you actually need."
- When to lock vs. float: "Should you lock your rate today? My take."
- Rate impact on payment: "What a 0.5% rate difference costs you over 30 years"

**Format:**
- LinkedIn: 12-slide carousel PDF — slide 1 hook, slides 2-10 data/insight, slide 11 TL;DR, slide 12 CTA
- Instagram: 30-second Reel — hook in first 3 seconds, data point, actionable takeaway, CTA

**Compliance requirements (MANDATORY):**
- NMLS# 513013 on every post in this pillar
- If specific rate mentioned → APR disclosure required
- No "guaranteed approval" language
- No "best rates guaranteed" language

**Caption formula (LinkedIn):**
"Rates moved [direction] this week. Here's what it means for Austin buyers. 🧵 Swipe for the breakdown. NMLS# 513013"

## Pillar 2: Austin Market Data

**Audience:** Austin homebuyers, realtors, local community
**Goal:** Establish Adam as the Austin-specific mortgage authority
**Platforms:** LinkedIn (primary), Instagram (carousel), Facebook (cross-post)
**Frequency:** 1 LinkedIn + 1 Instagram post per week = 2 posts (publish Friday)
**Data source:** Unlock MLS (unlockmls.com/stats) — pull weekly, free

**Content types:**
- Weekly market snapshot: "Austin market this week — [inventory], [median price], [days on market]"
- County breakdown: "Travis vs. Williamson vs. Hays County — where are buyers finding deals?"
- Buyer's market alert: "Austin now has 6.5 months of inventory. What that means for you."
- Price movement: "Austin median is 19% below the May 2022 peak. Is now the time to buy?"

**Format:**
- LinkedIn: 10-slide carousel — stat + plain-English interpretation per slide, CTA on last slide
- Instagram: Carousel or static image with bold stat + 2-line caption

**Data sourcing workflow:**
1. Visit [unlockmls.com/stats](https://www.unlockmls.com/stats) every Thursday
2. Pull: active listings, median price, days on market, months of supply
3. Compare to prior week
4. Write Friday post based on biggest data point movement

## Pillar 3: Buyer Education

**Audience:** First-time homebuyers, renters thinking about buying
**Goal:** Build trust with buyers before they need a LO; top-of-funnel awareness
**Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook
**Frequency:** 1 post per week (rotate platforms)

**Content types:**
- "5 myths about buying a home in 2026" (carousel)
- "How to get pre-approved in 24 hours" (Reel or carousel)
- "What credit score do you actually need for a mortgage?" (carousel)
- "FHA vs. Conventional — which is right for you?" (carousel)
- "Down payment assistance programs in Texas — what's available in 2026" (LinkedIn + Instagram)

**Format:**
- LinkedIn: Carousel (education + credibility signal for B2B audience)
- Instagram: Reel (myth vs. fact format for discovery)

**CTA:** "Apply now: mslp.my1003app.com/513013/register" or "DM me your questions"

## Pillar 4: Realtor Resources

**Audience:** Realtors and transaction coordinators in Austin metro
**Goal:** Generate referral partner relationships; become realtors' go-to LO
**Primary Platform:** LinkedIn only (B2B audience, professional context)
**Frequency:** 1 LinkedIn post per week

**Content types:**
- "What every Austin realtor needs to know about pre-approvals this quarter"
- "How to close a deal faster — the pre-approval timing trick most realtors miss"
- "Your clients keep losing offers. Here's why and how to fix it."
- "Interest rate buydowns explained — how to use them as a negotiating tool"
- "Co-marketing compliant social post templates for realtors" (referral-safe, RESPA compliant)

**Format:** LinkedIn text post or carousel

**Compliance note:** Co-marketing content must be distributed to general public — not targeted at specific consumers in an existing relationship.

**CTA:** "Send your buyers to get pre-approved before their first showing. Forward this. NMLS# 513013"

## Pillar 5: Personal Brand

**Audience:** Everyone — humanizing content builds trust with all audience segments
**Goal:** Differentiate Adam as an authentic person, not a corporate account
**Platforms:** Instagram (primary), LinkedIn (text-only), Facebook
**Frequency:** 1 post per week

**Content types:**
- Behind-the-scenes: "What a closing day looks like from the LO side"
- Austin community: "Favorite coffee shops to take client calls in Austin" or "Austin housing market field report — I drove [X] neighborhoods this week"
- Faith/values angle (as appropriate): "Why I got into mortgage" or "How I think about helping people build wealth through homeownership"
- Client milestones (with permission, no specific rates): "Client closed today. First home. 6 months of prep. Worth it."
- Lessons learned: "The biggest mistake I see buyers make in this market" (raw, conversational)

**Format:**
- Instagram: Phone-shot short video (30–60 sec) — no production value needed
- LinkedIn: Text-only post, 150–300 words, no image

**Compliance note:** Still include NMLS# 513013 in bio/profile at all times. If any rate or loan terms are referenced, full compliance disclosures required.
