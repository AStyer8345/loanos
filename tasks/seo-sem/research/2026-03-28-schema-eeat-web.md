## 1. FAQPage Structured Data — Google Search Central
URL: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/faqpage
Summary: FAQ rich results are now restricted to government and health-focused authoritative websites only. Mortgage/financial sites will not receive FAQ rich result display in SERPs. However, proper FAQPage markup still benefits AI systems (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) that parse structured data to cite and summarize content. Best practice: only apply FAQPage schema to pages that exist primarily to answer questions.

## 2. Schema Markup Guide 2026 — Backlinko
URL: https://backlinko.com/schema-markup-guide
Summary: Structured data signals are increasingly important for AI-driven search (AI Overviews, SGE) even when traditional rich results are restricted. For mortgage sites, the highest-ROI schema types in 2026 are: LocalBusiness, FAQPage (for AI citation), BreadcrumbList, AggregateRating, and Article for blog posts. Schema does not directly boost rankings but helps content surface in AI-generated answer boxes.

## 3. E-E-A-T for YMYL Content — Ahrefs
URL: https://ahrefs.com/blog/eeat-seo/
Summary: Mortgage content is explicitly classified YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) by Google. YMYL sites face stricter quality evaluation — Google's ranking algorithms give more weight to Authoritativeness and Trust signals for financial queries. Key E-E-A-T actions for mortgage sites: (1) add author bio with NMLS number and credentials to every blog post, (2) cite sources and official data, (3) build backlinks from industry publications, (4) ensure accurate and up-to-date content — outdated rate info or wrong compliance language can tank trust signals.
