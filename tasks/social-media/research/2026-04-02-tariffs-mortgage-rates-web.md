## The Tariff Paradox — The Story to Tell

Trump's sweeping "Liberation Day" tariffs triggered an unexpected short-term mortgage rate drop:

**Short-term (happening NOW):**
- Tariff uncertainty → investors flee to Treasury bonds (safe haven)
- Bond demand spikes → yields drop → mortgage rates fall
- Result: Rates at 6.25%, down from recent highs

**Long-term risk (the caveat):**
- If tariffs raise consumer prices → inflation spikes → Fed pauses rate cuts
- Higher inflation = Fed holds rates higher for longer
- Mortgage rates could reverse and climb back up
