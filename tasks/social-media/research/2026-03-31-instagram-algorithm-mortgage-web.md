# Research — Instagram Algorithm 2026 + Mortgage LO Social Posting
Date: 2026-03-31
Source: Hootsuite (blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/)
Topic: Instagram algorithm tips 2026 — financial services cadence and engagement

## Key Findings

### Instagram Algorithm Priorities (2026)
- Shares via DM are the **most heavily weighted signal** for distribution — content that gets shared privately travels further in the algorithm than content that just gets likes
- Originality is rewarded: Instagram pushes content made for Instagram, not recycled from other platforms (TikTok watermarks are actively penalized)
- Completion rate matters more than duration — shorter Reels are more likely to be watched in full, which boosts reach

### Financial Services Posting Cadence
- Best times for financial services on Instagram: **Wednesdays 8–10 AM**, Fridays at 10 AM (Sprout Social data)
- Highest engagement rate for financial services: **4.64%** at 26 posts/week (high-frequency outlier — not realistic for a solo LO)
- For a solo professional, 2 posts/week is the more achievable sweet spot: real estate adjacent accounts average 4.23% engagement at that frequency

### Real Estate + Mortgage Video Stats
- 73% of potential clients are more likely to work with agents who use video
- Reels average 3.0% engagement for real estate accounts
- Short-form Reel ideas that convert: Q&A content, rate myth-busting, behind-the-scenes loan process, local market takes

### Compliance Note (RESPA)
- Co-marketing reels/flyers with realtors require clear disclosure of the affiliate relationship
- Uneven cost-splits in joint ads can be treated as disguised referral payments under RESPA Section 8
- CFPB $1.75M penalty in 2023 for illegal agent incentives — the enforcement precedent is set and active

## Gaps This Research Fills
- Confirms DM-share strategy: posts with a "send this to someone buying a home" CTA should outperform saves/likes
- Validates 2x/week cadence for Instagram as realistic and high-engagement for mortgage LOs
- Flags RESPA co-marketing risk for any posts created with realtor partners
