## 3-Sentence Summary

Search Engine Land reports AI Overview CTR climbed from 1.3% (Dec 2025) to 2.4% (Feb 2026) per Seer Interactive — an 85% rebound in two months, suggesting users are starting to click through summaries again. Meta descriptions now act as a compressed intent signal in AEO content: they reinforce who the page is for, the problem it solves, and the framing — making them more important, not less, for AI-influenced search. Citations beyond position 4–5 in an AI Overview decay sharply, so the play is to be the cited source, not just an indexed page.
