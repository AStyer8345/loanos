### Post 194 — LinkedIn Personal — Thu Jan 21 9 AM CT (15:00 UTC)

I caught myself this week running the math on my best year ever.

24 deals in a month. Over $10 million in volume. The drive home after a $10k day — I can still feel it.

That’s the version my brain replays.

What it edits out: the missed dinners. The 11pm emails. The conversations I had with clients that should have been with my family. The texts I never sent. The Sunday afternoons I spent staring at a phone instead of being with the people I built it all for.

The highlight reel feels like the truth, but it’s a movie I directed. I cut the scenes I don’t want to remember. Then I judge today against a fiction.

I’m trying something different in 2027. Same numbers if they come, but no awards for the cost.

Some seasons measure success by what you accomplish. Some seasons measure it by what you protect.
