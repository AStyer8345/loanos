# Web Research — LinkedIn & Facebook Algorithm Updates (PM Session)
Date: 2026-03-28

## Source 1: Sprout Social — Facebook Algorithm 2026
URL: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/facebook-algorithm/
Summary: Facebook's October 2025 update means its recommendation engine now surfaces ~50% more Reels from creators who published that day, making freshness critical. Organic reach is rebounding from lows of 1-2% as Meta shifts to "interest media" — AI-powered distribution beyond your follower base. Content that gets shared via DMs is the strongest signal to the algorithm for small business accounts.

## Source 2: Buffer — LinkedIn Marketing Complete Guide 2026
URL: https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-marketing/
Summary: LinkedIn in 2026 rewards expertise-first content — educational carousels, thought leadership, and native video outperform link posts significantly. The algorithm actively suppresses outbound links in the body of posts; keep links in comments instead. Video posts earn 84% more engagement than text-only posts, making short Reels-style clips (15-90 sec) the highest-ROI format for LOs.

## Source 3: Buffer — LinkedIn Video Guide
URL: https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-video/
Summary: LinkedIn's dedicated video feed (launched 2025) surfaces short-form video to users who haven't connected with you, creating organic discovery opportunity comparable to Instagram Reels. Best practice: post native video (not YouTube links), vertical 9:16 format, captions mandatory (85% watch without sound). For mortgage LOs, "day in the life" and quick rate education clips under 60 seconds perform best.
