## Post 164 — Full Copy ("DTI kills deals (not the rate)")
Platform: LinkedIn | Pillar: education | Scheduled: 2026-10-16 09:00 AM CST

Buyers blame the rate when the deal falls apart.

Usually, it is not the rate.

It is DTI.

Debt-to-income ratio decides whether you qualify before a single rate quote gets sent. Most buyers have no idea what number they are working with.

The formula: all monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income times 100.

Take a $60k salary with $800/month in student loans and a car note. You are probably at 42-43% DTI before the mortgage even exists. Conventional loans cap at 45%. FHA goes higher — but so does the cost.

The fix is not a lower rate. The fix is lower debt payments.

I have seen buyers increase their purchase power by $80k from paying off one credit card. Took three months.

The math is not complicated. The conversation is usually overdue.

DM me DTI and I will show you exactly where you stand.
