## Source 2: Instagram Best Practices 2026 — Clicks to Conversions
URL: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-best-practices/
Domain: Sprout Social (authoritative)

Key takeaways:
- Carousels outperform single images AND Reels for engagement in finance niche
- Best posting time for financial services: 9-10am Wednesday (feed), 5am Monday (Reels)
- Mix of formats: 40% Reels (reach), 40% carousels (engagement), 20% static (brand consistency)
- Stories with polls/questions build audience relationship faster than feed posts

Relevance to Adam: The 30-day calendar should weight carousels for engagement, Reels for reach. Wednesday 9am and Monday 5am are the optimal slots.
