## Architecture Notes

### Dual DnD Libraries
- `@hello-pangea/dnd` (contacts page kanban) AND `@dnd-kit/core` + `@dnd-kit/sortable` (loans detail, contacts column ordering) are both installed and imported
- Both are used in `contacts/page.tsx` simultaneously
- Tech debt — should consolidate to `@dnd-kit` only, which is more actively maintained
- Not a quick fix due to complexity of the kanban board

### Two Stage Normalization Systems
- `src/lib/stageNormalization.ts` — handles **contact** stages (Lead, Pre-App, In Process, Closed, Other) — 8 aliases, used by contacts page, import route, bulk-action route, quick-add route
- `src/lib/constants/loan-stages.ts` — handles **loan** statuses (StageKey system, 50+ aliases) — comprehensive, canonical
- These are NOT redundant — they serve different domains (contact stages vs loan statuses)
- However, they look similar at first glance and could confuse future contributors
- Recommendation: rename `stageNormalization.ts` → `contactStageNormalization.ts` to clarify purpose
