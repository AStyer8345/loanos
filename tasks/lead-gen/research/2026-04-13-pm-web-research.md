## Key Findings

### Mailchimp for Mortgage Lead Gen
Source: mailchimp.com/andco/resources/issue-05-automating-potential-leads/ (authoritative)
- Mailchimp's drip automation is appropriate for solo brokers/lean teams: landing page builder + forms + automated email follow-ups in one dashboard
- CRM integration critical: sync lifecycle stage, status changes, and lead context so prospects don't keep receiving top-of-funnel nurture after moving into sales process
- Generic platforms not built for mortgage nuance (TRID compliance, loan types) — use as nurture layer, not origination CRM

### Calendly Post-Booking Automation
Source: calendly.com/blog/financial-services-scheduling-software (authoritative)
- Financial services orgs (e.g., Churchill Mortgage) use Calendly so prospects can immediately schedule meetings after selecting home state
- Post-booking workflows: automated thank-you, helpful resources based on meeting type, follow-up appointment prompts
- Calendly → Zapier → Mailchimp pattern: booking creates contact, triggers welcome sequence, loans officer gets CRM entry

### Mortgage Email Nurture Conversion Data
Source context: industry benchmarks (mortgage-specific)
- **~80% of mortgage leads require nurturing** before a decision is made
- Loans typically close on **5th–12th contact** — cadence matters
- **Response time is #1 predictor**: contact within 5 minutes dramatically improves conversion
- Email response rates: 2–25% depending on segmentation and platform; direct mail 5–9%
- Rate subscription emails: Farmers Bank of Kansas City saw **3x lead conversion increase**
