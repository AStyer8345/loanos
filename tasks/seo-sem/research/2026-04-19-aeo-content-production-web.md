## Summary (3 sentences)
To produce AEO-friendly content that gets cited by AI engines, structure pages with descriptive H2/H3 headings, short paragraphs, and direct answers immediately following question-based headers — avoiding over-optimization by not making every H2 a question. Schema types like FAQPage, HowTo, and LocalBusiness help machines identify which content fragments to pull into AI-generated answers. For local service businesses like mortgage brokers, the key is pairing structured headings with first-party proof signals (named cities, specific rates, named programs) that AI systems can verify against other trusted sources.
